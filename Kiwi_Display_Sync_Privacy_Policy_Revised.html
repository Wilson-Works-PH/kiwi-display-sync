This Privacy Policy explains how Kiwi Technologies ("Kiwi," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information through the Kiwi Display Sync app ("the App"), available on Google Play. Kiwi Display Sync is a companion app used together with Kiwi digital display hardware and is designed to work with Kiwi CMS (cms.kiwi.com.ph), our cloud-based content management platform — content, schedules, and device status you manage in the App sync with your Kiwi CMS account. By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy, and where the law requires your affirmative consent, we obtain that consent separately as described in Section 4.

1. Developer Information and Contact

Kiwi Display Sync is developed and published by Kiwi Technologies, based in the Philippines.

For privacy inquiries, data requests, or concerns, contact us at:

  • Email: info@kiwi.com.ph
  • Phone: +63 969 170 2299
  • Office Hours: Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM

2. Privacy Inquiries and Concerns

You may reach our Data Privacy Officer at dpo@kiwi.com.ph for any concern that general support cannot resolve, including data subject requests and complaints about how your information is handled.

3. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect:

  • Account information — name, email address, and business/company details used to log in and manage your Kiwi Display Sync account, shared with your linked Kiwi CMS account.
  • Device and display data — device identifiers, pairing status, connectivity status, and display performance data needed to sync and manage your screens.
  • Content you upload — images, videos, GIFs, PDFs, and other files you upload for display on your Kiwi screens.
  • Usage data — app interactions, scheduling activity, and diagnostic logs used to maintain and improve the App.
  • Support communications — information you share with us when requesting technical support, including screenshots or files you choose to send.

We collect this information directly from you when you create an account, upload content, or contact support, and automatically from your device and displays as they connect and sync with the App and with Kiwi CMS.

We do not knowingly collect information from children, and the App is not directed to children. See Section 13 for more on this.

4. Basis for Processing and Your Consent

We process your personal data on one or more of the following bases: your consent, the performance of our contract with you (providing the App, Kiwi CMS, and related services), our legitimate business interests (such as security and service improvement), and compliance with legal obligations.

Where consent is our basis for processing — for example, before support staff access uploaded images, videos, or device logs beyond what's needed for basic troubleshooting — we ask for that consent as a specific, affirmative step, not as a condition buried in general terms of use. You may withdraw consent at any time using the contact details in Section 1; doing so may limit our ability to provide certain support or features that depend on that processing.

5. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App, Kiwi CMS, and their services
  • Sync content and manage device/display status in real time between the App and Kiwi CMS
  • Provide technical support and troubleshoot issues
  • Maintain security and prevent unauthorized access
  • Improve app performance, features, and user experience
  • Communicate service updates, billing notices, or support responses

6. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information only in the following cases:

  • Within Kiwi's own services — your account and content data sync between the App and your Kiwi CMS account (cms.kiwi.com.ph), since both are operated by Kiwi Technologies to run the same displays
  • Service providers — trusted vendors (e.g., cloud hosting) who process data on our behalf, under confidentiality obligations
  • Support access — our authorized personnel may access uploaded images/videos and device logs strictly for support and troubleshooting purposes
  • Legal requirements — when required by law, regulation, or valid legal process
  • Business transfers — in the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, subject to continued protection of your data

7. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers protected by technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the data, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit, and encryption of sensitive data at rest
  • Access controls limiting staff access to personal data on a need-to-know basis
  • Monitoring and logging designed to detect unauthorized access

While we take data security seriously, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account remains active. Once you close your account, we delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it longer to comply with a legal obligation, resolve a dispute, or enforce our agreements. Support communications and diagnostic logs are retained for no longer than 6 months from creation.

9. Account and Data Deletion

If you have created a Content Management System (CMS) account, you can request deletion of your account and its associated data at any time, in either of these ways:

  • In the App — go to Settings > Account > Delete Account and confirm your request.

On the web — visit [Insert dedicated account-deletion page URL, e.g. https://kiwi.com.ph/delete-account, or confirm https://kiwi.com.ph/contact/ as the interim request page] or email info@kiwi.com.ph with your account details and "Delete my account" in the subject line.

We will delete your account and associated personal data — not merely deactivate it — within 30 days of a verified request, except for data we are required to retain for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance, which we retain only as long as those reasons apply. If your account is linked to a paid subscription, you may need to cancel billing separately before deletion completes; we'll tell you if that applies to you.

10. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, including the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), you have the right to:

  • Be informed that your personal data will be, is being, or has been processed
  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion or blocking of your data
  • Object to processing, including processing for direct marketing
  • Data portability — receive a copy of your data in a portable, commonly used format
  • Withdraw consent for data processing, where applicable
  • Be indemnified for damages sustained due to unlawful processing of your data
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission if you believe your rights have been violated

To exercise these rights, contact us or our DPO using the details in Sections 1 and 2.

11. Marketing Communications

We will only send you marketing or promotional information if you ask us to — for example, by emailing or calling us to request it. You may opt out of marketing communications at any time using the contact details in Section 1, without affecting your ability to use the App.

12. Data Breach Notification

If we become aware of a security incident that compromises your personal data, we will assess the risk of harm and, where the law requires it, notify the National Privacy Commission and affected users within 72 hours of learning of the breach, or as soon as practicable thereafter. Our notification will describe the nature of the incident, the data involved, and the steps we are taking in response.

13. Children's Privacy

The App is a business tool for managing digital displays and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we learn that a child has provided us with personal data, we will take reasonable steps to delete it. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided us with personal data may contact us using the details in Section 1.

14. Permissions Used by the App

The App may request device permissions (e.g., network access, storage) solely to enable core features such as device pairing, content download, and display syncing. We do not use these permissions to collect data beyond what is necessary for the App's functionality.

15. Regulatory Compliance

Kiwi Technologies is registered under Wilson Works Trading Inc. with the National Privacy Commission (NPC), Registration No. PIC-005-743-2026, in compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations.

16. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will reflect material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date above, and where changes materially affect your rights, we will also notify active account holders directly via email before the change takes effect. Continued use of the App and CMS platform after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
